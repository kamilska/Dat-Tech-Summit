Local Essentials & Highlights

Wawel Dragon

Steps from Sheraton: the bronze dragon statue breathes real fire every few minutes by the Vistula river.

Hourly Bugle Call

Every hour from St. Mary’s Basilica tower, the Hejnał trumpet melody stops mid-note honoring a 13th-century watchman.

Currency & Cards

Polish Złoty (PLN). Contactless cards, Apple Pay & Google Pay work everywhere—even for single coffees and trams.

Local Must-Tries

Try a warm braided Obwarzanek bagel from a blue street cart, classic Pierogi, and mulled wine on the Main Square.
