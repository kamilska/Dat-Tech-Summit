Local Essentials

Wawel Dragon

Steps from Sheraton: the bronze dragon statue breathes real fire every few minutes by the Vistula river.

Hourly Bugle Call

Every hour from St. Mary’s Basilica tower, the Hejnał trumpet melody stops mid-note in honor of a 13th-century watchman.

Currency & Cards

Polish Złoty (PLN). Contactless cards, Apple Pay & Google Pay work everywhere—even for single coffees and trams.

Local Must-Tries

Grab a warm braided Obwarzanek bagel from a blue street cart, traditional Pierogi, and mulled wine at the Main Square.
