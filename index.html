Local Tips

🐉 Wawel Dragon

Right next to Sheraton Grand stands Wawel Castle—and a fire-breathing dragon statue at its foot.

🎺 St. Mary's Trumpet Call

Every hour on the Main Square, a live bugler plays the Hejnał melody from the higher tower.

💳 Cashless & PLN

Currency is Polish Złoty (PLN), not Euro. Contactless cards, Apple Pay & Google Pay work everywhere.

🥨 Local Flavors

Try a fresh braided Obwarzanek bagel from a street cart and traditional handmade Pierogi.
